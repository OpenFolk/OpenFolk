# Code of conduct

OpenFolk follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks, discrimination and publishing others' private information are not tolerated anywhere the project
happens, in issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and events.

Reports go to **conduct@openfolk.app**. They are handled confidentially by the maintainers, who may warn, limit
or remove participation as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
